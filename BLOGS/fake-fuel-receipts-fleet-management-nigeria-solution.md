## The Hidden Mechanics of Fuel Invoice Fraud in West Africa

Traditional fleet accounting relies on paper receipts, physical logbooks, or company-issued fuel debit cards. However, corrupt practices have evolved far beyond simple lost receipts:

1. **Inflated Pump Receipts:** Drivers arrange with filling station attendants to print receipts reflecting 300 liters when only 200 liters were actually delivered into the tank.
2. **Side-Tank Offloading:** Fuel is pumped into the truck tank under full receipt verification, but the driver stops 15 minutes down the road to drain 50 liters into commercial jerrycans sold on the black market.
3. **Receipt Swapping & Collusion:** Drivers submit discarded receipts collected from third-party vehicles or buy blank stamped receipt books to fabricate claims entirely.

When fleet managers attempt to cross-check these expenses against mileage, drivers blame severe Lagos traffic jams, heavy container delays at Apapa Port, or rough unpaved road conditions across Ogun and Oyo states.

## How Automated IoT Fuel Reconciliation Eliminates Fraud

WizIOT eliminates reliance on paper claims by establishing an automated, tamper-proof verification chain between physical tank volume sensors, GPS location data, and telematics accounting software.

### Step 1: Real-Time Fuel Level Influx Verification
When a truck pulls into a filling station, the in-tank capacitive probe detects fuel level rise with ±0.2% accuracy. The telematics unit logs the precise volume added (e.g., 214.5 Liters), the exact GPS coordinates of the station, and the start/stop timestamps of the refueling event.

### Step 2: Automated Receipt & Card Matching API
When the driver submits a fuel card transaction or paper receipt claim, WizIOT automatically reconciles the claimed volume against the sensor-verified tank increase. If a receipt claims 300 liters but the tank sensor recorded only 214.5 liters added, the software flags an immediate **Fuel Short-Fill Exception**.

### Step 3: Geofenced Filling Station Verification
Unapproved roadside fuel vendors often sell adulterated diesel mixed with kerosene or water, damaging high-pressure common rail (HPCR) truck injectors. WizIOT geofences authorized filling stations (e.g., TotalEnergies, Mobil, Conoil depots). Refueling events occurring outside authorized geofences trigger an automated compliance alert.

## 3 Key Telematics Controls for Nigerian Fleet Operations

### 1. Sub-Liter Tank Volume Resolution
Factory float sensors cannot detect 30-liter short-fills due to wide dead-band zones. WizIOT immersion capacitive probes measure fuel levels continuously, identifying volume changes down to 0.5 liters.

### 2. CAN-Bus Engine Consumption vs. Distance Audit
By tapping directly into the vehicle's engine control unit (ECU via J1939 CAN-bus), WizIOT tracks exact engine fuel burn per kilometer. Comparing actual engine fuel burn against claimed fuel purchases exposes ghost mileage claims instantly.

### 3. Immediate Driver Scorecard Impact
Driver fuel variance scores are calculated automatically. Drivers maintaining zero fuel variance receive monthly efficiency bonuses, while those with repeated invoice mismatches are flagged for audit.
