## The Technical Vulnerabilities of Unpowered Trailer Tracking

Standard commercial vehicle telematics devices rely on constant 12V/24V power from the truck engine alternator. When a trailer is unhooked, three technical failures occur:

1. **Rapid Battery Depletion:** Small internal lithium backup batteries inside standard trackers last only 4 to 8 hours during continuous location reporting, leaving dropped trailers unmonitored over weekends.
2. **Loss of Temperature Feeds:** Wireless BLE temperature sensors inside unhooked trailers cannot relay data if the primary tractor gateway is powered down or driven away.
3. **Yard Misplacement & Asset Hoarding:** Unmonitored trailers are easily misplaced in large port yards, or hoarded by customers beyond agreed free-demurrage windows without billing tracking.

## 4 Engineering Components of Solar-Powered Trailer Telematics

### 1. Heavy-Duty Solar-Rechargeable Lithium Gateway

WizIOT solar-powered trailer gateways incorporate high-efficiency monocrystalline solar panels bonded to industrial IP69K waterproof enclosures mounted on trailer roofs or front walls.

- **Continuous Solar Re-Charging:** Monocrystalline solar cells recharge the internal industrial battery pack using ambient daylight—even on overcast or winter days.
- **180-Day Zero-Sun Power Reserve:** High-capacity Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) internal battery maintains up to 180 days of continuous location and temperature logging in total darkness (e.g., inside covered shipping warehouses).

### 2. Autonomous Gateway BLE Receiver Array

The solar gateway operates as a master Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE 5.0) hub, continuously reading temperature, humidity, and door contact sensors inside the unhooked trailer.

- **Uninterrupted Cold Chain Logging:** Even when the tractor unit is unhooked and driven 500 km away, the solar trailer gateway continues logging BLE temperature sensor feeds every 5 minutes, uploading data to cloud servers.
- **Door Breach Security:** Detects and alerts on unauthorized door openings occurring while trailers are parked in staging yards.

### 3. Smart Yard Geofencing & Demurrage Billing Automation

WizIOT software automatically monitors trailer dwell time in logistics yards and port terminals:

- **Automated Arrival & Departure Logs:** Records exact timestamps when trailers enter or exit customer yards.
- **Automated Demurrage Alerts:** Triggers notifications when dropped trailers exceed contractual free-storage windows (e.g., > 48 hours), automatically generating detention billing invoices.

### 4. IP69K Ruggedized Low-Profile Enclosure

Trailer roofs endure extreme weather, high-speed wind drag, tree branch strikes, and automatic trailer wash stations.

WizIOT solar gateways feature low-profile (22 mm thickness) polycarbonate housings with UV-stabilized solar covers, certified to **IP68 / IP69K waterproof and shock standards** (-40°C to +85°C operating range).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: Do WizIOT solar gateways charge under cloudy or winter weather conditions?**  
Yes. High-efficiency monocrystalline solar cells generate electrical current from ambient UV light, maintaining battery charge even under cloudy, rainy, or Scandinavian winter conditions.

**Q2: How is the solar gateway mounted on container trailers?**  
WizIOT solar gateways mount using industrial 3M VHB structural adhesive tape or self-tapping stainless steel rivets on trailer front walls or roof crowns, installing in under 15 minutes.

**Q3: Can the solar gateway alert dispatchers if a trailer is stolen from a yard at night?**  
Yes. WizIOT solar gateways include 3-axis accelerometers that detect unauthorized trailer movement or hitching during night curfew hours, transmitting immediate **Yard Theft Alarms**.

**Q4: How frequently does the solar unit ping location coordinates while parked in a yard?**  
While parked stationary in a yard, the unit transmits location pings every 4 hours to conserve power, automatically switching to 1-minute motion pings the instant the trailer moves.
