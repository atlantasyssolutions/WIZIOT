## The Operational Crisis: EV & Battery Analytics in GCC

Commercial electric delivery vans and heavy EV trucks operating in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and Riyadh face extreme thermal stress during summer months when pavement temperatures exceed +65°C. High ambient heat combined with rapid DC fast charging causes accelerated capacity fade, cell imbalance, and thermal runaway risks.

Unmanaged operational risks in this sector lead to severe financial shrinkage, legal liability, and insurance premium spikes. Transport operators require engineering-grade telematics hardware backed by automated compliance logging.
