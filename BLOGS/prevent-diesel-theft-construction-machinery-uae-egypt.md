## Why Off-Road Construction Machinery is Vulnerable to Fuel Theft

Unlike highway commercial trucks, heavy construction equipment operates under distinct operational conditions that invite fuel theft:

1. **Large Tank Capacities & High Volume Drains:** Heavy excavators and wheel loaders carry 400L to 800L fuel reservoirs, while continuous 500 kVA generators draw from 1,000L external belly tanks—making large siphoning events easy to disguise.
2. **Off-Hours Site Isolation:** Construction sites are largely vacant between 7 PM and 5 AM, giving dishonest personnel hours of unobserved access to parked equipment.
3. **Vague Engine Hour Accounting:** Manual operator logbooks record vague engine hours, allowing operators to claim that diesel was consumed during "heavy hydraulic digging" or "extended engine idling."

The software automatically calculates expected fuel usage:

$$\text{Expected Fuel Burn} = \text{Engine Hours} \times \text{Average Liters/Hour}$$
