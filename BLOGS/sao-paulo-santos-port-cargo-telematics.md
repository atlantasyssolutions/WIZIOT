# Cargo Theft Security & Cold Chain Telematics out of Port of Santos, Brazil

The freight corridor connecting São Paulo to the Port of Santos—Latin America's largest container port—is a critical artery for international trade. Millions of tons of coffee, beef, poultry, electronics, and pharmaceuticals transit the Rodovia dos Imigrantes and Via Anchieta highways annually.

However, transport operators in São Paulo face dual operational threats: high rates of highway cargo hijacking by organized criminal syndicates, and strict GDP temperature compliance requirements for perishable agricultural and pharmaceutical exports.

### 1. Highway Cargo Hijacking & Signal Jamming
Armed hijacking gangs operate along highway rest stops and port access roads, using powerful RF jammers to disconnect standard vehicle trackers before forcing drivers onto secondary roads.

### 2. Cold Chain Refrigeration Spoilage
Export containers carrying frozen meat, fruit, or vaccines are susceptible to reefer diesel generator failure, improper setpoint configuration, or door tampering during port staging queues.
