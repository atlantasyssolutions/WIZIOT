# EU GSR 2024 Compliance: Intelligent Speed Assistance (ISA) & Telematics Mandates

The implementation of the European Union's General Safety Regulation (GSR 2024 / Regulation EU 2019/2144) marks a major regulatory shift for commercial vehicle operators across Europe. As of July 2024, all newly registered commercial trucks, buses, and delivery vans in the EU must be equipped with mandatory Intelligent Speed Assistance (ISA) systems, Event Data Recorders (EDR), and Driver Drowsiness and Attention Warning (DDAW) tech.

For cross-border haulage companies operating across Germany, Poland, Romania, Hungary, and Spain, meeting ISA compliance is no longer optional—it is a legal prerequisite for fleet operation, vehicle licensing, and EU transport audits.

---

## What is Intelligent Speed Assistance (ISA)?

Intelligent Speed Assistance (ISA) is an onboard safety system that alerts drivers when they exceed legal posted speed limits or automatically restricts engine throttle input to maintain compliance.

### Mandatory Technical Features Required Under EU GSR 2024:
1. **Optical Speed Sign Recognition:** Forward cameras scanning roadside speed signs including dynamic electronic overhead signs.
2. **HD Digital Map Fusion:** GPS map layer cross-referencing variable regional speed limits when signs are obstructed by snow, rain, or heavy traffic.
3. **Cascading Driver Feedback:** Cascading audible, visual, and haptic (pedal pushback) warnings when speed limits are breached.
4. **Tamper-Evident Event Data Recording:** Automatic logging of vehicle speed, brake application, and driver interventions prior to critical incidents.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Is Intelligent Speed Assistance (ISA) mandatory for all commercial fleets in the EU?
Yes. As of July 2024, EU GSR 2024 requires ISA systems on all newly registered M1, M2, M3, N1, N2, and N3 category commercial vehicles operating within EU member states.

### Q2: How does WizIOT ISA handle speed limits in weather conditions that obscure signs?
When road signs are obscured by snow, fog, or mud, WizIOT automatically falls back to its onboard HD GPS map database to maintain accurate local speed limit awareness.

### Q3: Can a driver turn off the ISA system?
Under EU regulations, drivers can temporarily override or switch off ISA feedback during a trip, but the system must automatically re-enable every time the ignition is cycled on.

### Q4: Does ISA automatically apply the brakes on a truck?
No. Standard ISA systems provide audible/visual warnings or reduce throttle input to prevent acceleration. They do not initiate emergency service braking unless integrated with Autonomous Emergency Braking (AEB).

### Q5: How does WizIOT ISA sync with digital tachographs?
WizIOT plugs directly into the K-Line / CAN interface of digital tachographs, automatically archiving speed, driver card IDs, and driving hours into encrypted cloud compliance storage.
