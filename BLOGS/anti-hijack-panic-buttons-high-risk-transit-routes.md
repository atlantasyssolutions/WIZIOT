## Why Visible Dashboard Panic Buttons Put Drivers at Risk

Many generic telematics installers fit cheap red push-buttons directly on the center dashboard console.

Visible dashboard panic buttons create critical operational security flaws:
1. **Immediate Hijacker Retaliation:** Hijackers entering the cab immediately notice red emergency buttons; seeing a driver reach for the dashboard often triggers immediate physical assault.
2. **Accidental False Triggers:** Exposed dashboard buttons are easily bumped by drivers reaching for paperwork or coffee cups, generating constant false alarms.
3. **Loss of Access:** Once a driver is removed from the cab or forced into the sleeper berth, dashboard buttons become completely inaccessible.

A professional anti-hijack emergency system requires concealed, multi-location silent triggers that drivers can activate without visual detection.
