## What Determines Fuel Monitoring Installation Costs in Kenya?

Fuel monitoring telematics pricing is driven by 4 core technical hardware and service components:

```
Total Per-Truck Investment = [Fuel Sensor Hardware] + [Telematics Gateway Unit] + [Installation & Tank Calibration] + [Monthly Data Subscription]
```

### 1. Fuel Sensor Type: Capacitive Probes vs. Mechanical Floats
- **Mechanical Float Arm Sensors (Low Cost, High Error):** Factory float sensors cost KES 8,000 to KES 15,000 but carry a wide ±15% measurement error margin. They cannot detect 40-liter siphoning events.
- **Digital Capacitive Immersion Probes (High Precision):** Immersion capacitive probes cost KES 28,000 to KES 45,000 per tank. They deliver sub-liter resolution with ±0.2% measurement precision across 500L to 1,000L tanks.

### 2. Single Tank vs. Dual Tank Configurations
Heavy commercial trucks (e.g., Mercedes Actros, Scania, Isuzu FYR) frequently carry dual fuel tanks (500L + 500L) connected by an equalizer pipe. Dual-tank setups require 2 calibrated capacitive probes and a multi-tank differential summation gateway.

### 3. Telematics Gateway Features (4G LTE, Anti-Tamper, CAN-Bus)
Basic 2G GPS trackers cost KES 12,000 to KES 18,000 but freeze during border crossings. Professional 4G LTE telematics gateways equipped with CAN-bus J1939 ECU protocols, multi-SIM roaming, and armored anti-tamper wiring cost KES 35,000 to KES 55,000.

### 4. Certified Field Installation & 10-Point Tank Calibration
Proper tank calibration requires adding fuel in precise 50-liter increments while logging sensor voltage curves. Certified field installation and 10-point tank calibration range from KES 12,000 to KES 20,000 per vehicle.
