## Why Built-In Reefer Unit Alarms Fail to Protect Food Cargo

Most refrigerated trailers (Thermo King, Carrier Transicold) feature local control panels with red warning lights and audible buzzers. However, local alarms fail in transit for four common operational reasons:

1. **Cab Noise Insulation:** Modern truck cabs feature high soundproofing; drivers traveling at 80 km/h cannot hear local alarm buzzers mounted on the front exterior wall of trailer units.
2. **Reefer Power System Stalls:** If the reefer generator stalls or battery power fails, the entire local alarm circuit dies, producing no warning signal whatsoever.
3. **Overnight Sleep Dead-Zones:** Drivers sleeping at rest stops along the N1 or N3 do not see warning lights on exterior trailer panels until morning.
4. **Driver Tampering:** In dishonest operations, drivers occasionally turn off cooling units overnight to reduce diesel consumption, turning them back on shortly before arrival to fool manual arrival checks.
