## How Criminal Syndicates Theft Bulk Fuel in Transit

Bulk fuel tankers present distinct security vulnerabilities compared to standard freight haulers. Theft syndicates exploit several physical weaknesses:

1. **Manifold Discharge Valve Cracking:** Drivers crack open compartment discharge valves while parked on quiet highway laybys, draining fuel into hidden underground storage tanks or mobile tankers.
2. **Top Loading Hatch Tampering:** Security seals on top manhole covers are carefully cut, fuel is siphoned out, and duplicate counterfeit seals are reapplied before arrival.
3. **Internal Compartment Short-Filling:** Drivers tamper with compartment internal emergency foot-valves, trapping 500 liters inside specific compartments during offloading, which is later drained and sold at unauthorized yards.

Traditional manual seal auditing fails because corrupt drivers obtain matching counterfeit seal numbers or report seals as "broken in transit due to road vibration."

## 4 Engineering Controls for Bulk Fuel Tanker Security

### 1. Smart Electronic Compartment Valve Locks

WizIOT bulk tanker security replaces mechanical padlocks and plastic seals with heavy-duty electronic compartment valve locks wired directly into the vehicle telematics gateway.

- **Geofenced Unlocking Logic:** Discharge valves remain physically locked and electronically disabled during transit. They can only be unlocked when the tanker enters authorized GPS destination geofences (e.g., specific petrol station depots).
- **Encrypted RFID Driver Authentication:** Offloading requires authorized driver RFID keycard authentication paired with control room confirmation.

### 2. Top Manhole Hatch & Bottom Valve Contact Sensors

Every compartment top manhole cover and bottom discharge valve is fitted with IP68 waterproof magnetic contact switches and optic sensors.

- **Real-Time Hatch Opening Alarms:** If a top manhole hatch or bottom discharge valve is opened outside an authorized destination geofence, WizIOT hardware transmits an immediate **Unauthorized Compartment Breach Alarm** via SMS and control room alerts within 3 seconds.

### 3. Electronic Fuel Dipstick Telemetry (Overfill & Drop Sensing)

High-precision electronic level sensors installed in each tanker compartment monitor liquid level continuous height in real time.

- **In-Transit Drop Detection:** If liquid volume drops in any compartment while the tanker is in motion or parked on a highway shoulder, the system flags an immediate **Bulk Fuel Drop Event** pinned to exact GPS coordinates.

### 4. Automated Depot Weighbridge & Flow Meter Integration

WizIOT platform software integrates directly with depot flow meters and weighbridges, cross-referencing initial refinery loading volumes against destination offload flow rates. Any discrepancy exceeding 0.1% generates an automated compliance exception report.
