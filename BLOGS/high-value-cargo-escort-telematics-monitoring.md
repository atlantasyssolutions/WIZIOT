## The Vulnerabilities of Unlinked Convoy Operations

Traditional high-value transport relies on verbal radio communication and visual line-of-sight between the cargo truck driver and the security escort team. However, unlinked convoy operations suffer from three major security vulnerabilities:

1. **Visual Separation in Traffic:** Heavy urban traffic, traffic signals, or highway congestion frequently separate the cargo truck from the trailing escort vehicle by several hundred meters, creating unmonitored hijack windows.
2. **RF Jamming Communication Interruption:** Criminal rings use localized RF jammers to block handheld two-way radio channels and cellular phones simultaneously, preventing drivers from calling for help.
3. **Control Room Blindness:** Dispatch platforms viewing separate vehicle markers cannot evaluate inter-vehicle distance automatically, failing to detect when an escort vehicle falls behind.

## 4 Engineering Components of WizIOT Convoy Telematics

### 1. Inter-Vehicle Proximity Telemetry & Dynamic Geofencing

WizIOT convoy security establishes a dynamic, moving geofence buffer (Proximity Envelope) centered around the primary cargo truck.

- **Automated Distance Auditing:** Continuous BLE 5.0 / Ultra-Wideband (UWB) wireless ranging monitors the exact distance between the cargo truck and escort security vehicles.
- **Proximity Separation Alarms:** If the separation distance between the cargo truck and escort vehicle exceeds pre-configured limits (e.g., >150 meters) for longer than 30 seconds, WizIOT triggers an immediate **Convoy Separation Alarm** in both vehicle cabs and at central dispatch.

### 2. Dual-Path Inter-Vehicle RF Mesh Communications

To maintain communication during cellular or radio jamming attacks, WizIOT convoy hardware incorporates short-range, encrypted RF mesh data links (868 MHz / 915 MHz ISM band) connecting the cargo truck directly to the escort vehicle.

- **Cellular-Independent Security Sync:** Panic button triggers, door breach alerts, or jammer detection signals in the cargo truck instantly transmit to the escort vehicle over the RF mesh link, bypassing jammed cellular networks.
- **Automated Escort Cab Siren:** If the cargo truck suffers a door breach or anti-jam alarm, high-decibel sirens activate inside the escort vehicle cab within 0.5 seconds.

### 3. Synchronized Dual-Vehicle Mobile Engine Immobilization

WizIOT convoy software allows tactical commanders inside the trailing escort vehicle to trigger remote safe engine immobilization on the cargo truck if a hijack is observed visually.

- **Biometric Tactical Control:** Escort commanders use FaceID-authenticated mobile tablets to issue a safe **Limp-Mode Cut-Off** command to the cargo truck, bringing it to a controlled stop before hijackers can drive it away.

### 4. Integrated Dual-Camera Live Video & Audio Feeds

WizIOT ADAS dashcams mounted on both cargo and escort vehicles stream real-time dual video feeds to central dispatch and mobile tactical tablets.

- **360-Degree Perimeter Visibility:** Forward, rear, and cabin cameras deliver live HD video feeds during convoy transit, automatically saving high-priority video clips to secure cloud servers during panic or separation alarms.
