## The Regulatory Reality of WHO GDP Compliance in West Africa

The World Health Organization (WHO) and Nigeria's NAFDAC (National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control) enforce strict Good Distribution Practice guidelines for temperature-sensitive medical products:

1. **Continuous Temperature Mapping:** Storage environments must maintain continuous 24/7 temperature logging with data sampling intervals no greater than 5 minutes.
2. **Multi-Zone Temperature Verification:** Large reefer trailers require multi-point temperature sensing to detect thermal hot-spots near doors and cooling vents.
3. **Tamper-Proof Audit Records:** Temperature records submitted for compliance must be immutable, electronically signed, and protected against manual alteration.

Manual paper charts and basic single-point reefer gauges fail all three requirements, exposing carriers to immediate cargo rejection, license suspension, and insurance claim denials.
