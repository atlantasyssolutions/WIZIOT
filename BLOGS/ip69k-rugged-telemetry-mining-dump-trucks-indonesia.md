## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q: What is the difference between IP67 and IP69K ingress protection?
IP67 protects against temporary water immersion up to 1 meter. IP69K protects against intense high-pressure (100 bar), high-temperature (80°C) steam jet washdowns.

### Q: Why is resin potting critical for mining telematics?
Solid potting locks all circuit board components in place, eliminating solder joint fatigue caused by continuous heavy machine vibration.

### Q: How does WizIOT handle cellular blackouts inside deep open-pit mines?

### Q: Can WizIOT track hydraulic system health on excavators?
Yes. By monitoring hydraulic oil pressure and temperature via CAN-bus, WizIOT detects pump wear before catastrophic failure occurs.
