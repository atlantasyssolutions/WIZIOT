# Heavy Haulage Telematics for Mountainous Transport Corridors in Colombia

Commercial freight haulage in Colombia operates over some of the most challenging terrain in South America. Negotiating steep mountain passes across the Cordillera Oriental between Bogotá, Medellín, and the Pacific port of Buenaventura places extreme mechanical stress on heavy truck engines, transmission systems, and service brakes.

Additionally, long transit times through remote mountain corridors leave haulage fleets vulnerable to fuel siphoning, unauthorized detours, and cellular dead zones.

1. **Engine Thermal Abuse & Overheating:** Prolonged low-gear climbing under heavy loads causes engine oil and coolant temperatures to spike, risking catastrophic engine seizure.
2. **Undetected Diesel Theft:** Drivers siphoning fuel during mountain rest stops while blaming low fuel economy on steep gradients.
3. **Mountain Cellular Blackouts:** Signal loss in deep valleys preventing real-time tracking updates.
