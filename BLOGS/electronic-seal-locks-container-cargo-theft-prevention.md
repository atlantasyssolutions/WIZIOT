## The Vulnerabilities of Disposable Container Bolt Seals

Standard ISO 17712 high-security bolt seals are widely used in shipping, but they suffer from three critical operational vulnerabilities:

1. **Zero Real-Time Breach Alerting:** Disposable seals cannot alert dispatchers when a breach occurs; tampering is only discovered days later at final delivery.
2. **Seal Number Counterfeiting:** Organized theft rings manufacture replica bolt seals with matching serial numbers, replacing cut seals during transit.
3. **Hinge & Latch Bypassing:** Thieves frequently unbolt door hinge pins or latch brackets directly, opening container doors without breaking the door lock seal at all.

To secure intermodal containers, fleet operators require smart electronic seal locks that integrate real-time GPS telemetry, door contact sensors, and geofenced unlocking authorization.

## 4 Engineering Components of Smart Electronic Seal Locks

### 1. Heavy-Duty Steel Cable Lock Mechanism with Fiber-Optic Core

WizIOT smart electronic seal locks replace disposable bolts with heavy-duty, cut-resistant steel cable locks featuring internal fiber-optic continuity loops.

- **Fiber-Optic Cut Detection:** If the steel cable is cut or tampered with, the internal fiber-optic light loop breaks instantly, triggering an immediate **Electronic Seal Breach Alarm** via cellular/satellite telemetry within 3 seconds.
- **Reusable Industrial Housing:** Housed in IP68 waterproof, shock-resistant poly-carbonate enclosures designed for multi-year reuse across thousands of container trips.

### 2. Geofenced Authorized Unlocking Logic

Smart electronic seal locks cannot be unlocked manually or with physical keys during transit.

- **Geofenced Unlocking Authorization:** The lock's internal motor mechanism remains electronically locked throughout transit. It can only be unlocked when the container enters pre-authorized GPS destination geofences (e.g., specific port terminals or warehouse yards).
- **Encrypted RFID & Mobile App Authentication:** At the destination geofence, offloading personnel unlock the seal using encrypted RFID keycards or authorized mobile app authentication.

### 3. Integrated Multi-Sensor Door Breach Telemetry

Securing the seal cable alone is not enough if thieves unbolt door hinges. WizIOT smart electronic seals incorporate:
- **3-Axis Accelerometer Motion Sensing:** Detects violent door tampering and vibration impacts.
- **Ambient Light Intrusion Sensors:** Detects light entering the container interior during unauthorized door openings.
- **Wireless Door Contact Sensors:** Monitors exact door open/close timestamps and duration.

### 4. Continuous GPS & Satellite Container Tracking

WizIOT smart electronic seal locks house independent GPS receivers and multi-carrier cellular modems, operating as standalone asset trackers.

- **Long-Life Battery Performance:** Internal high-capacity LiSOCl2 batteries provide up to 5 years of tracking (2 pings daily) or 90 days of high-frequency transit tracking.
- **Tamper-Proof Audit Certificates:** Generates single-click PDF compliance reports showing complete container route trails, door-opening timestamps, and geofenced unlock verifications for customs and cargo insurers.
