# Container Chassis & Unpowered Trailer Tracking with Solar BLE Telematics

Managing intermodal container chassis, flatbed trailers, and unpowered drop-deck trailers across busy port terminals, rail ramps, and customer distribution yards is a notorious operational headache. Without internal battery power from a tractor unit, unpowered assets frequently become "lost" in large staging yards, leading to missed customer pickups, low asset utilization, and unnecessary lease costs.

Modern intermodal asset tracking requires self-powered, solar-rechargeable telematics hardware capable of surviving extreme weather, ocean saltwater spray, and heavy coupling vibration while broadcasting location coordinates for over 10 years.

## 4 Engineering Pillars of WizIOT Solar BLE Asset Telematics

### 1. High-Efficiency Solar Panel & LiFePO4 Battery Architecture
WizIOT chassis trackers feature integrated monocrystalline solar panels paired with high-capacity Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) rechargeable batteries. Engineered for extreme operating temperatures (-30°C to +75°C), the unit recharges fully with just 2 hours of indirect sunlight, providing continuous maintenance-free operation for up to 10 years.

### 2. BLE Wireless Cargo & Kingpin Pairing
Wireless Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) sensors installed on the trailer kingpin and door latches detect when a tractor couples to the chassis or when trailer doors are opened. The gateway automatically pairs tractor telematics with trailer asset IDs.

### 3. Automated Geofence Dwell Time & Demurrage Billing
WizIOT cloud software automatically tracks entrance and exit timestamps at ports, rail yards, and customer facilities. If a chassis exceeds free dwell time thresholds, automated demurrage billing alerts are generated.

### 4. IP68 / IP69K Saltwater & Steam Washdown Enclosures
Housed in UV-stabilized, impact-resistant polycarbonate enclosures, WizIOT asset trackers withstand saltwater immersion at seaport docks and high-pressure 100-bar steam cleaning.

Without any sunlight, the internal LiFePO4 battery powers the asset tracker for up to 120 days under standard 2-pings-per-day reporting schedules.

### Q2: How are WizIOT chassis trackers mounted to prevent theft or removal?
Trackers are mounted to chassis structural beams using tamper-proof huck bolts or heavy-duty huck rivets that require specialized pneumatic tools to remove.

### Q3: Can BLE sensors monitor container temperature when mounted on a chassis?

Yes. WizIOT asset trackers utilize global multi-band LTE-M (Cat-M1) and NB-IoT modems with 2G fallback, ensuring seamless tracking across global ports.
