## Why Marine Cargo Insurers Reject Cold Chain Spoilage Claims

Cargo insurance adjusters scrutinize temperature spoilage claims to identify carrier negligence. Common reasons for claim denial include:

1. **Gaps in Temperature Records:** Manual logbooks or USB loggers with unrecorded time windows fail to prove cooling continuity.
2. **Suspected Manual Falsification:** Unencrypted spreadsheets (Excel/Word) submitted post-incident are routinely dismissed as potentially altered documents.
3. **Unverified Third-Party Liability:** Insurers refuse payouts if the carrier cannot prove whether cargo was ruined during road transit or prior to loading at the supplier's warehouse.
4. **Lack of Maintenance Proof:** If the carrier cannot prove that the reefer unit was properly maintained and operational before departure, insurers cite "unseaworthy equipment" clauses.
