## Why Built-In Reefer Unit Alarms Fail in Transit

Most commercial refrigerated trailers (Thermo King, Carrier Transicold) feature built-in temperature displays and local buzzer alarms. However, local alarms fail in real-world transport for several critical reasons:

1. **Driver Inattention or Noise:** Heavy engine noise and highway cabin vibration make audible reefer buzzers difficult to hear, especially while drivers are sleeping at truck stops.
2. **Reefer Power Interruption:** If the reefer unit generator stalls or loses battery power, the local alarm unit dies alongside the cooling system—emitting no alarm whatsoever.
3. **Driver Tampering to Save Fuel:** In dishonest operations, drivers occasionally turn off reefer compressor units during long night hauls to save diesel fuel, turning them back on an hour before arrival to fool manual temperature checks.
4. **Wired Sensor Corrosion:** Traditional wired temperature probes mounted inside cargo bays suffer cable fatigue, wire breaks, and sensor drift over time.
