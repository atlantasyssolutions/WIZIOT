# Dual-AI DMS Cameras: Stopping Driver Fatigue & Distraction in Long-Haul Fleets

Long-haul commercial transport operators face severe safety risks driven by driver fatigue, micro-sleep episodes, and mobile phone distractions. Traditional telematics systems rely solely on accelerometer data—detecting hard braking or harsh cornering *after* a dangerous maneuver has already occurred.

Modern video telematics shifts fleet safety from reactive incident logging to proactive collision prevention. By integrating dual-camera AI units featuring Driver Monitoring Systems (DMS) alongside Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS), safety managers gain real-time visibility into in-cabin behavior and road hazards simultaneously.

1. **Micro-Sleep Episodes:** Unintentional eye closure lasting 1.5 to 3 seconds while traveling at 90 km/h covers up to 75 meters with zero driver control.

## 4 Engineering Pillars of Dual-AI Video Telematics

### 1. Infrared Pupil Tracking & Facial Mesh Analysis
WizIOT DMS cameras utilize infrared LEDs and 60 FPS optical sensors to track driver eye gaze, blink duration, pupil dilation, and head orientation—even through polarized sunglasses and in complete cabin darkness.

### 2. Real-Time Edge Processing & Instant Audible Alerts
Computer vision inference models run locally on the camera's Neural Processing Unit (NPU). When micro-sleep, prolonged eye diversion (>2 seconds), or phone usage is detected, the unit triggers an immediate high-decibel cabin alarm in under 200 milliseconds to snap the driver back to attention.

### 3. Integrated ADAS Road Hazard Correlation
DMS alerts are correlated with forward-facing ADAS camera telemetry. If a fatigue alert coincides with rapid lane drift, tailgating, or forward collision warnings, the event severity is automatically escalated in the safety portal.

### 4. Privacy-Preserving Blur & Encrypted Dual-Storage
To comply with global data privacy regulations (such as EU GDPR), WizIOT video telematics encrypts all footage onboard. Continuous recording remains local on tamper-proof industrial SD/SSD storage, while short 10-second HD video clips are uploaded to the cloud only during verified safety events.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Does the DMS camera record driver conversations or private cabin audio?
No. Audio recording can be disabled at the firmware level. WizIOT DMS focuses strictly on optical computer vision facial mesh metrics.

### Q2: How does the system perform when drivers wear dark sunglasses?
WizIOT DMS uses active 940nm infrared illumination, which penetrates dark lens coatings to track pupil motion and eye closure accurately.

### Q3: How are video clips uploaded over cellular networks?
Only short 10-second clips (5 seconds before and 5 seconds after an event trigger) are compressed and uploaded over LTE/5G, keeping data SIM usage minimal.

### Q4: Can drivers disable or block the camera lens?
If a driver covers or rotates the camera lens, the unit detects optical occlusion and immediately issues a tamper alarm to fleet dispatchers.
