# ADAS Forward Collision Warning Systems for Highway Freight Fleets

Commercial heavy trucks require significantly longer stopping distances than passenger cars. A fully loaded 40-ton tractor-trailer traveling at 90 km/h requires over 100 meters to come to a complete stop under emergency braking. When forward traffic slows suddenly or a vehicle cuts into a truck's lane, a fraction of a second in reaction time determines the difference between a routine stop and a fatal collision.

Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) powered by forward-facing AI cameras provide continuous headway distance monitoring, lane tracking, and forward collision warnings, giving drivers the crucial seconds needed to brake safely.

### Primary ADAS Safety Functions:
1. **Forward Collision Warning (FCW):** Calculates real-time Time-to-Collision (TTC) based on relative vehicle speed and distance. Triggers urgent audible alerts when TTC falls below 2.7 seconds.
2. **Headway Monitoring & Warning (HMW):** Alerts drivers when following distance behind a lead vehicle drops below safe headway thresholds (e.g., 1.5 seconds).
3. **Lane Departure Warning (LDW):** Detects unintentional lane drifting or swerving when turn signals are not activated, preventing highway run-off-road crashes.
4. **Pedestrian Collision Warning (PCW):** Identifies pedestrians and cyclists in front of urban delivery vehicles during low-speed maneuvers.

### 1. High-Dynamic Range (HDR) Optical Vision
WizIOT ADAS cameras utilize HDR optical sensors capable of recognizing lane markings, vehicle tail-lights, and obstacles under challenging lighting conditions—including direct sun glare, heavy fog, pouring rain, and unlit night highways.

### 2. Time-to-Collision (TTC) Dynamic Calculation
Rather than relying on fixed static distance metrics, WizIOT ADAS continuously calculates dynamic Time-to-Collision based on real-time vehicle speed telemetry from the CAN-bus. As speed increases, safety warning zones expand proportionally.

### 3. Dual-Camera ADAS + DMS Event Correlation
WizIOT correlates forward ADAS warnings with in-cabin DMS driver attention data. If an ADAS headway warning occurs while the driver's eyes are focused away from the road, the alert severity is automatically elevated and logged as a high-risk safety event.

### 4. EU GSR 2024 Regulatory Compliance

WizIOT ADAS cameras detect vehicles and obstacles up to 150 meters ahead under daylight conditions and up to 100 meters under nighttime headlight illumination.

### Q2: Does ADAS automatically apply the brakes on heavy trucks?
Standard WizIOT ADAS provides high-decibel audible and visual warnings to prompt driver braking. It can be integrated with Autonomous Emergency Braking (AEB) systems where supported by vehicle CAN-bus architecture.
