## The 4 Financial Variables of Fleet Fuel ROI

Calculating fuel monitoring telematics ROI requires measuring 4 core operational variables:

1. **Fleet Size ($N$):** Total number of active commercial vehicles/trucks in the fleet.
2. **Average Monthly Fuel Spend per Vehicle ($F_{\text{monthly}}$):** Average fuel expenditure per truck per month.
3. **Historical Fuel Loss Percentage ($L_{\text{percent}}$):** Estimated percentage of fuel lost to siphoning, short-fills, and invoice fraud (industry average ranges from 12% to 22%).
4. **Total Telematics Capital Investment ($C_{\text{capex}}$):** One-time cost of fuel sensors, telematics hardware, installation, and tank calibration per truck.

## The Core Telematics ROI & Payback Formulas

### Formula 1: Monthly Fleet Fuel Savings ($S_{\text{monthly}}$)

Assuming an 85% to 90% fuel theft elimination efficiency post-WizIOT deployment:

$$S_{\text{monthly}} = N \times F_{\text{monthly}} \times L_{\text{percent}} \times 0.85$$

### Formula 2: Total One-Time Fleet Capex ($T_{\text{capex}}$)

$$T_{\text{capex}} = N \times C_{\text{capex}}$$

### Formula 3: Hardware Payback Period in Months ($P_{\text{months}}$)

$$P_{\text{months}} = \frac{T_{\text{capex}}}{S_{\text{monthly}} - (N \times M_{\text{opex}})}$$

Where $M_{\text{opex}}$ is the monthly SIM data and cloud software subscription fee per vehicle.

### Formula 4: First-Year Net ROI Percentage ($\text{ROI}_{1\text{yr}}$)

$$\text{ROI}_{1\text{yr}} = \left( \frac{(S_{\text{monthly}} \times 12) - T_{\text{capex}} - (N \times M_{\text{opex}} \times 12)}{T_{\text{capex}}} \right) \times 100$$

## Worked Financial Case Study: 50-Truck Commercial Fleet

Let me illustrate these formulas using real commercial benchmarks for a 50-truck freight transport fleet:

### Operational Input Data:
- Fleet Size ($N$): **50 Trucks (Dual-Tank Configuration)**
- Monthly Diesel Spend per Truck ($F_{\text{monthly}}$): **$3,200**
- Estimated Fuel Loss Rate ($L_{\text{percent}}$): **15%**
- One-Time Hardware + Install Cost per Truck ($C_{\text{capex}}$): **$950**
- Monthly SaaS & SIM Fee per Truck ($M_{\text{opex}}$): **$20**

### Step-by-Step Financial Calculation:

#### 1. Gross Monthly Fuel Savings:
$$S_{\text{monthly}} = 50 \times \$3,200 \times 0.15 \times 0.85 = \mathbf{\$20,400 \text{ / Month}}$$

#### 2. Total Initial Capex Investment:
$$T_{\text{capex}} = 50 \times \$950 = \mathbf{\$47,500}$$

#### 3. Monthly Net Cash Flow (After SaaS Fees):
$$\text{Net Monthly Cash Flow} = \$20,400 - (50 \times \$20) = \mathbf{\$19,400 \text{ / Month}}$$

#### 4. Hardware Payback Period:
$$P_{\text{months}} = \frac{\$47,500}{\$19,400} = \mathbf{2.45 \text{ Months (74 Days)}}$$

#### 5. First-Year Net Profit Impact & ROI:
$$\text{First-Year Net Cash Benefit} = (\$19,400 \times 12) - \$47,500 = \mathbf{\$185,300}$$

$$\text{First-Year Net ROI} = \left( \frac{\$185,300}{\$47,500} \right) \times 100 = \mathbf{390.1\% \text{ Net ROI}}$$
