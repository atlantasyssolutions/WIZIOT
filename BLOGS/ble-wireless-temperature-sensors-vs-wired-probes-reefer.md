## Technical Comparison: Wired Temperature Probes vs. BLE Wireless Sensors

### 1. Traditional Wired Temperature Probes
Traditional cold chain setups use resistive temperature detectors (RTD Pt100 or thermistors) connected to a central telematics gateway via long copper wires routed through trailer walls.

- **Installation Complexity:** Requires drilling holes through reefer thermal insulation walls, sealing grommets, and running 12-meter cable conduits along trailer ceiling rails.
- **Physical Vulnerabilities:** Cables freeze, embrittle, and fracture under sub-zero temperatures. Forklifts, cargo pallets, and steam washdowns frequently snag and snap exposed wires.
- **Thermal Leak Hazards:** Drilling cable access holes through trailer insulation degrades thermal integrity over time, creating localized moisture ingress and insulation rot.

### 2. Modern BLE 5.0 Wireless Temperature Sensors
BLE wireless sensors incorporate a precision digital temperature sensor, a Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE 5.0) radio transmitter, and an industrial lithium battery inside a sealed IP68 waterproof housing.

- **Zero-Cable Installation:** Mounts directly on internal trailer walls using industrial adhesive backing or two stainless steel screws in under 5 minutes.
- **Autonomous Battery Operation:** Powered by internal industrial Lithium-Thionyl Chloride (LiSOCl2) batteries operating continuously for 3 to 5 years.
- **IP68 / IP69K Waterproofing:** Fully encapsulated in industrial potting resin, withstanding high-pressure steam cleaning and chemical sanitization inside food trailers.

## 4 Operational Advantages of BLE Wireless Cold Chain Sensors

### 1. Rapid 5-Minute Installation (Zero Trailer Wall Drilling)

Wired sensor installation takes 2 to 3 hours per trailer, requiring specialized mechanics to drill insulation walls and route cable conduits. BLE wireless sensors install in under 5 minutes per trailer, eliminating installation downtime and preserving factory trailer insulation warranties.

### 2. Multi-Zone Temperature Tracking Without Wiring Complexity

Modern refrigerated logistics frequently requires multi-compartment trailers carrying distinct frozen (-20°C), chilled (+4°C), and ambient (+15°C) zones separated by movable bulkheads.

Installing wired sensors across movable bulkheads is near impossible. BLE wireless sensors can be mounted anywhere—on movable bulkheads, inside individual cargo pallets, or on rear door frames—transmitting data wirelessly to the primary telematics gateway in the tractor unit.

### 3. Extended 3-to-5 Year Battery Life in Extreme Cold

A common concern regarding wireless sensors is battery life in sub-zero environments. WizIOT BLE 5.0 sensors utilize specialized industrial **LiSOCl2 battery chemistries**:

- **Extreme Cold Stability:** Delivers stable voltage and full signal transmission at -30°C freezer temperatures.
- **Ultra-Low Power Protocol:** BLE 5.0 sleep-wake cycles ensure up to 5 years of continuous temperature logging (sampling every 60 seconds).

### 4. Seamless Pallet-Level & Container Cold Chain Tracking

Because BLE sensors are wireless and compact, they can be placed directly inside high-value pharmaceutical cargo boxes or export fruit pallets.

- **Pallet-Level Telemetry:** Measures exact internal product temperature rather than ambient trailer air temperature.
- **Cross-Docking Tracking:** BLE gateways at warehouse docks scan incoming pallet sensors automatically during unloading, logging seamless cold chain handover trails.
