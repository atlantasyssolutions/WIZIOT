## The Risks of Unmonitored Night-Time Vehicle Movement

Leaving commercial vehicles unmonitored overnight creates four severe operational and financial risks:

1. **Unauthorized Night Siphoning & Fuel Theft:** Vehicles started overnight are driven to quiet laybys where hundreds of liters of diesel are siphoned into black-market containers.
2. **Uninsured Accident Liability:** Drivers operating company vehicles outside authorized working hours expose the business to catastrophic third-party liability claims if involved in night-time collisions.
3. **Premature Mechanical Wear & Asset Depreciation:** Unapproved night usage adds thousands of unrecorded kilometers to odometers, accelerating brake, clutch, and tire replacement cycles.
4. **Yard Theft & Off-Hours Hijacking:** Vehicles parked in unmonitored yards are prime targets for overnight break-ins, battery theft, and yard hijacking.

## 4 Engineering Components of Night Curfew Telematics

### 1. Time-Windowed Dynamic Curfew Geofencing

WizIOT telematics software allows fleet managers to establish automated **Time-Windowed Curfew Rules** customized for individual vehicles or entire fleet groups:

- **Configurable Time Schedules:** Set active curfew windows (e.g., Monday through Friday from 8:00 PM to 5:30 AM, and 24-hour weekend curfews).
- **Out-of-Hours Movement Alarms:** If a vehicle ignition is turned ON or if movement exceeding 50 meters occurs during active curfew hours, WizIOT triggers an immediate **Curfew Breach Alarm** via SMS, WhatsApp, and control room push notifications within 5 seconds.

### 2. Automated Night Curfew Ignition Lockout Relays

Preventing unauthorized movement is superior to reacting post-movement. WizIOT telematics gateways connect directly to vehicle starter solenoids and ignition relays:

- **Automated Night Lockout:** During active curfew hours, the gateway automatically engages an internal electronic ignition lock.
- **Biometric Over-the-Air (OTA) Override:** If an emergency night shift requires vehicle movement, authorized managers issue a temporary 1-hour ignition override directly from the WizIOT mobile app using FaceID authentication.

### 3. Depot Yard Perimeter Geofence Monitoring

Establish virtual high-resolution polygon geofences around company depots, maintenance yards, and driver residential parking zones.

- **Depot Exit Alarms:** If a vehicle leaves the designated depot yard boundary during curfew hours, the platform generates high-priority **Yard Breach Alerts** pinned to exact GPS coordinates.
- **Speed & Route Violation Logging:** Logs full speed profiles and street route maps for any unauthorized out-of-hours trip.

### 4. Automated Out-of-Hours Mileage & Fuel Variance Reporting

WizIOT software automatically separates daytime business trips from out-of-hours movement:
- **Out-of-Hours Mileage Audits:** Calculates exact kilometer distance driven outside approved work schedules.
- **Fuel Consumption Reconciliation:** Cross-references CAN-bus fuel burn against curfew travel, generating automated payroll deduction reports for unauthorized fuel usage.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: What happens if a vehicle is already on the road when the night curfew window begins?**  
WizIOT safety logic will not stall a moving vehicle. The platform logs an **Overdue Curfew Alert**, engaging ignition lockout only after the vehicle comes to a complete stop and the ignition is turned off.

**Q2: Can night curfew rules be customized for different days of the week?**  
Yes. WizIOT software supports fully customizable weekly schedules, allowing different curfew start/stop times for weekdays, Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays.

**Q3: Does ignition lockout work if a driver disconnects the truck battery?**  
Yes. WizIOT gateways incorporate internal backup batteries and bistable latching relays; if main battery power is cut during curfew hours, the ignition relay remains locked.

**Q4: Can curfew alerts be routed to third-party security guarding companies?**  
Yes. WizIOT API webhooks can route out-of-hours yard breach alerts directly to site security guarding services for immediate physical depot inspection.
