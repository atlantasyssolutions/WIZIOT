## The Biological Realities of Fresh Seafood Cold Chain Logistics

Fresh fish cold chain logistics operates under strict biological constraints:

1. **The 0°C to +2°C Ice-Slurry Boundary:** Fresh (non-frozen) fish must be maintained at exact 0°C to +2°C temperatures in melting ice slurry. A temperature rise of just 3°C doubles bacterial growth rates.
2. **Histamine Risk Above +4°C:** Exposure to temperatures above +4°C for as little as 2 hours triggers irreversible histamine formation, leading to immediate rejection by European (EFSA) and US (FDA) food safety inspectors.
3. **High Salt & Moisture Corrosion:** Seafood transport environments feature high humidity, melting ice saltwater runoff, and salt air corrosion that rapidly destroys standard wired telematics sensors.
