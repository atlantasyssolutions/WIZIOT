## The Danger of Crude Engine Kill Switches

Many traditional anti-theft installers fit basic relay switches that instantly cut power to the truck starter solenoid or fuel pump upon receiving a remote SMS command.

While this sounds effective, crude instant cut-off relays create severe hazards:
- **Highway Collisions:** Sudden engine shutdown while traveling at 80 km/h on a busy highway can lock power steering and air brakes, causing multi-vehicle pileups.
- **Accidental Triggering:** False SMS triggers caused by network glitches can stall trucks in high-speed lanes.
- **Driver Assault Risks:** Sudden immobilization in isolated areas without security response integration exposes drivers to retaliation.

A professional enterprise remote engine immobilizer must execute a multi-stage, safety-verified immobilization protocol.

## 4 Engineering Pillars of Safe Remote Engine Immobilization

### 1. Controlled Speed Reduction (Limp-Mode Cut-Off)

WizIOT's intelligent remote immobilizer does not cut engine power abruptly at high speeds. When a hijacking alarm is verified, the system sends a command to the vehicle ECU to enter **Limp Mode**, capping engine speed to 15 km/h.

As the vehicle slows down safely onto the road shoulder, the system engages complete ignition lock once speed drops below 5 km/h.

### 2. Post-Ignition Restart Blocking

If hijackers manage to stall or turn off the ignition after hijacking the truck, the immobilizer prevents engine re-crank regardless of key status, keyless fob signals, or hot-wire attempts.

### 3. Concealed Foot-Pedal Panic Switches

Drivers facing armed threats require silent alarm activation. WizIOT installs concealed, heavy-duty foot pedal switches inside the driver footwell. Pressing the switch for 3 seconds transmits an immediate **Silent Hijack Emergency Signal** to the fleet control room and armed response dispatchers without alerting hijackers.

### 4. Direct API Integration with Armed Response Networks

Vehicle recovery speed determines whether cargo remains intact. WizIOT software integrates via real-time webhooks directly with private armed response networks across South Africa (e.g., Netstar, Tracker, Fidelity ADT). Emergency coordinates, vehicle color, registration, and live tracking links are dispatched automatically within 5 seconds of alarm verification.
